    Features

    Welcome to EduWow!

    EduWow is your interactive learning portal where students can complete worksheets, track progress, receive detailed feedback, earn badges, and join collaborative groups. Teachers can manage content, provide comments, monitor progress, and award badges. Explore each feature to maximize your learning experience!

    🏠 Home

    Access your dashboard, see an overview of features, and navigate quickly to any section.

    📚 Worksheets

    Students can view, complete, and submit worksheets. Teachers can add new worksheets and provide feedback for each student.

    💬 Feedback

    Receive detailed comments from teachers to improve your work. Teachers can track student progress and leave guidance.

    📊 Progress Monitoring

    Teachers can monitor student performance, track progress over time, and provide personalized support when needed.

    🏅 Badges

    Earn badges for achievements and milestones, motivating you to explore and learn more!

    🤝 Collaborative Groups

    Join groups to collaborate on projects, share ideas, and learn together in a fun way.

    🎮 Activities

    Engage in interactive games designed to strengthen skills and make learning enjoyable.
